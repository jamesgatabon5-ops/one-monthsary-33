letter one · where it began

My love,

Somewhere between the first message and the first real laugh, my whole sky quietly rearranged itself. I didn’t notice it happening. I only knew that suddenly every good thing I saw, I wanted to tell you first.

I used to think love would arrive loudly, like fireworks. Instead it came like the first star at dusk. Soft, certain, and already there before I thought to look up. And now I can’t imagine a night without it.

Thank you for being gentle with my heart from the very beginning. You never rushed me, and you never made me feel like I had to be anything but myself.

Out of all the galaxies, I’m so glad I found my way to yours.

— yours, always

one more thing

One more thing, my love,

If I could go back to the first day, I wouldn’t change a thing. Not the nervous smiles, not the clumsy words, not the waiting. Every step led me here, to you.

I promise to keep choosing you on the easy days, and especially on the hard ones.

You are my favorite beginning.

letter two · warmth

Dearest,

Some people are exciting. Some people are comforting. You are somehow both. With you, even a quiet evening feels like a celebration, and even a bad day feels survivable.

You are my warm light in a cold room. When the world gets loud and I get tired, I think of you, and my shoulders drop, and I remember that I have a safe place to land.

I never knew home could be a person until you.

Thank you for being my softest place and my strongest reason to smile.

— yours, always

one more thing

Stay a little longer,

I love the small things most. The way you say my name, the way you listen like what I’m saying matters, the way you make me feel chosen without even trying.

I hope I do the same for you. I hope you feel how much room you have in my heart, because it is all of it.

Wherever you are, that’s where I feel at home.

letter three · tenderness

My darling,

Loving you feels like watching something bloom slowly, a little more every day, in a way I never want to rush. You make me softer, braver, and more honest than I knew I could be.

Even when we’re quiet, even when we’re apart, I feel you in the small moments. A song that sounds like you, a color that reminds me of your smile, a thought that begins with “I should tell them…”

You’ve taught me that love isn’t only the big moments. It’s the patience, the care, the gentle way you hold what matters.

Thank you for helping me grow, and for growing beside me.

— yours, always

one more thing

Listen closely,

I want you to know that I see you. The effort you hide, the worries you swallow, the love you give so generously. I see all of it, and I’m grateful for all of it.

You don’t have to be perfect for me. You just have to be you. That has always been more than enough.

My heart is safe with you, and yours is safe with me.

letter four · dreams

To my favorite person,

There are nights I look up and the sky feels endless, and I think about how impossible it is that out of everyone, everywhere, our paths crossed. It feels written in the stars, like something the universe meant to do.

You make ordinary nights feel like paintings. Even the ones where we say nothing, where we’re just two people under the same sky, wanting the same quiet future.

Because yes, I think about the future. I think about it with you in it. Your laugh in the next room, your hand in mine, a thousand small tomorrows that feel like magic.

Whatever comes, I want to dream it with you.

— yours, always

one more thing

Look up with me,

When life feels uncertain, I hold onto this: whatever happens, I’ve already been lucky enough to love you, and be loved by you.

That kind of luck doesn’t come twice. I won’t waste it.

You are the brightest star in my whole sky.

letter five · november

My love,

Five months. Five galaxies. And somehow, I’m still discovering new ways to love you. This month, the sky turned pink and blue, like the moment between day and night when everything feels softer and a little more magical. That’s what you are to me. The place where all my feelings meet.

Pink for the tenderness you bring out in me. Blue for the calm I feel when I’m with you. Together, they make a color I’ve only ever seen when I look at us.

Thank you for every message, every laugh, every patient moment. Thank you for staying through the days I wasn’t my best. Thank you for being the kind of person who makes love feel safe.

Five months in, and my heart still skips when I see your name.

— yours, always

one more thing

Before this month ends,

I want you to know that I’m not just in love with who you are today. I’m in love with who you’re becoming, and I’m so proud to be near you while it happens.

Here’s to us, to the months behind us, and to every galaxy still waiting to be found.

I love you, today and in every sky to come.
